FAQ

The details before you start.

Do you manage our Meta ads?

No. Creovelo handles research, concepts, copy, and static ad production. Your team or media buyer runs campaign setup, spend, and optimization.

What do you need to get started?

Product information, approved imagery, brand guidelines, and advertising priorities. Existing creative and customer feedback help. Sprint delivery begins after intake is complete.

How quickly will we receive the creative?

The Creative Testing Sprint is delivered within five business days after completed intake and receipt of the required brand assets.

Is the sprint a subscription?

No. The $1,250 Creative Testing Sprint is a one-time purchase. There is no monthly commitment and no obligation to move to the $2,500 monthly partnership.

Do you use AI in your process?

We use modern creative tools, including AI-assisted workflows where appropriate, alongside research, direction, and quality review. Every asset is checked for brand fit, clarity, and product accuracy.

Do you guarantee a specific ROAS or CPA?

No. Outcomes depend on offer, media buying, audience, and landing page. We deliver research-led creative and a clear testing brief, without promising a particular result.
