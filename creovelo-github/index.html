06 / A few details

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Do you manage our Meta ads?

Creovelo focuses on research, creative concepts, copy, and static ad production. Your team or media buyer handles campaign setup, spend, and optimization.

What do you need to get started?

Your product information, approved imagery, brand guidelines, and advertising priorities. Existing creative, customer feedback, and campaign results are helpful when available. Sprint delivery begins after intake is complete.

How quickly will we receive the creative?

The Creative Testing Sprint is delivered within five business days after completed intake and receipt of the required brand assets. Monthly work follows two scheduled creative deliveries, with the calendar agreed at kickoff.

Can we start with one project?

Yes. The $1,250 USD Creative Testing Sprint is a one-time engagement: four concepts, two static variations per concept, a testing brief, and one consolidated revision round. There is no obligation to move to a monthly partnership.

Do you use AI in your process?

We use modern creative tools, including AI-assisted workflows where appropriate, alongside customer research, creative direction, and quality review. Every asset is reviewed for brand fit, clarity, and product accuracy.

Do you guarantee a specific ROAS or CPA?

No. Advertising outcomes depend on your offer, media buying, audience, landing page, and other factors. We deliver thoughtful, research-driven creative and clear testing recommendations, without promising a particular result.
